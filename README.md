# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales & Performance Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](coffee_shop_dashboard.png)

## Project Overview

This project features an executive-level Tableau dashboard built to analyze transaction trends, product performance, store metrics, and peak operating hours for a retail coffee business over a 6-month period.

## Business Goals

* **Identify Revenue Drivers:** Find out which product categories generate the highest sales volume.
* **Optimize Staffing:** Pinpoint exact transactional peak hours to align shift scheduling.
* **Evaluate Store Performance:** Compare order volumes across all retail locations to measure consistent market demand.
* **Provide Actionable Insights:** Translate raw transactional data into strategic business decisions for management.

---

## Key Parts

### 1. Data Work (SQL)
Before visualizing, SQL was used to pull, filter, and organize raw transaction logs:
* **Getting Data:** Pulled transaction IDs, dates, times, quantities, and store IDs.
* **Sorting & Filtering:** Grouped transaction patterns by products and store locations.
* **Calculating Numbers:** Computed total revenue ($698,812), total cups sold (214,470), and average order value ($4.69).

### 2. Dashboard Design (Tableau)
Built a clean, executive-styled interactive report:
* **KPI Row & Takeaway Banner:** Highlights macro metrics alongside a core strategic takeaway banner.
* **2x2 Grid Layout:** Features best-selling products, daily sales trends, store performance columns, and hourly volume breakdowns.

---

## Key Findings

* **Product Dominance:** Coffee and Tea drive over 67% of total revenue.
* **Morning Rush:** Transaction volume peaks heavily and strictly between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.
* **Consistent Store Footprint:** Order volumes remain evenly distributed across all 3 stores (Hell's Kitchen, Astoria, Lower Manhattan).
* **Steady Growth:** Daily sales trend lines show a healthy, consistent upward trajectory from January through July.

---

## Business Impact

This project helps management optimize daily operations, staffing, and revenue strategies.

### Revenue Growth
* Highlights top-performing items so management can prioritize inventory and cross-selling bundles.

### Staffing Efficiency
* Uncovers exact morning rush windows to eliminate understaffing during high-volume hours and scale back during slow evening hours.

### Regional Equity
* Confirms balanced brand traction across all store branches.
